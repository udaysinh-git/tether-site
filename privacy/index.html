Privacy

Privacy policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

Tether is a browser extension and a local program made by Udaysinh Sapate at Kuro Labs. This policy covers both.

What Tether collects

Nothing. Tether has no server, no analytics and no telemetry, and the developer receives no data about you or your browsing.

What happens on your computer

  • The extension reads the content of a tab only after you share it, approve an agent's request for it, or turn on bypass mode.
  • It sends that content to the Tether program running on your own computer, at 127.0.0.1, which never accepts connections from other machines.
  • The program passes it to the AI agent you run, such as Claude Code or Codex, when that agent asks.
  • Settings, your pairing key, saved styles and scripts, recordings and watchers are stored on your computer, in the browser's extension storage and in the .tether folder in your home directory.

Your AI agent

Your agent is a separate program from a separate company. Whatever it reads from a tab you shared goes to its own model provider under that provider's terms, the same as the code and files you give it. Choose an agent and plan whose data terms you're comfortable with.

Optional features that reach the internet

These only happen if you set them up:

  • Watchers load the pages you ask them to check, with your cookies, from your browser or your computer.
  • Phone alerts send a watcher's message to the ntfy server and topic you choose.
  • Publishing a style or script uploads it as a secret GitHub Gist through your own GitHub account.

Your control

Stop sharing any tab from the status panel or the popup, press Stop everything to end all sharing, and delete stored data by removing the extension and the .tether folder. Recording masks passwords, card numbers and one-time codes, and settings can turn off recording of anything you type.

Children

Tether is a developer tool and isn't directed at children.

Changes

Changes to this policy are published at https://tether.udaysinh.me/privacy/ and in the project's repository.

Contact

Open an issue at https://github.com/udaysinh-git/tether/issues or ask in the Tether Discord at https://discord.gg/UcB8z8u4JP.
